Highlights

We Are Best In Our Field

Grown the traditional way, delivered the modern way. Here is what makes Ketan Organics a partner that farmers, brands and buyers trust.

  • 10+Years of organic farming
  • 4Product ranges
  • 100%Natural, no shortcuts

Every herb, grain and oil we offer is grown the natural way by our own farmer network — no chemical fertilisers, no synthetic pesticides — and checked for quality before it leaves the farm.

Our work creates steady, year-round income for farming families in our network — from cultivation and harvesting to processing, distillation and packing.

We guide our farmers in organic cultivation, soil care and post-harvest handling, so every season they grow better crops and earn a fairer price.

From seed to shipment, each batch is inspected for purity, freshness and consistency, so what reaches you is exactly what was promised.

Bulk and wholesale orders are packed to travel — clean, well-labelled and ready for buyers in India and overseas.
