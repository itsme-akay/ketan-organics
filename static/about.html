Our Story

Purely organic, from our farms to you

Ketan Organics began with a simple belief: food and wellness products should be grown the way nature intended. For over a decade we have worked hand in hand with our own network of farmers, following traditional organic practices — no chemicals, no shortcuts.

Today we grow, process and supply essential oils, grains, hydrosols and mentha products to brands, wholesalers and buyers in India and abroad. Every batch is checked for quality, freshness and purity before it leaves our hands.

How We Work

From Farm to You

Grown the traditional way, delivered the modern way. Four careful steps stand between the field and your order.

  1. Step 01

    Grow

    Crops are raised by our farmer network using traditional organic practices — healthy soil, natural inputs, no chemical shortcuts.

  2. Step 02

    Harvest

    Each crop is harvested at the right time and handled with care, so its natural aroma, colour and strength stay intact.

  3. Step 03

    Process

    Herbs are cleaned and dried, grains are sorted, and oils and hydrosols are steam-distilled in small, carefully watched batches.

  4. Step 04

    Check & Pack

    Every batch is checked for purity and consistency, then packed clean and well-labelled — ready for bulk, wholesale or export.

Our Farmer Network

Growing together with our farmers

Our farmers are partners, not suppliers. We work with the same families season after season, sharing knowledge on organic cultivation, soil care and post-harvest handling — and paying a fair price for the care they put into every crop.
