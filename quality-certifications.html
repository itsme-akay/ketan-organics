Quality & Certifications

Quality-checked at every step — from the farm where it is grown to the pack that reaches you.

Our Quality Promise

What you order is exactly what you get

Quality is not a final inspection for us — it runs through everything we do. Because we work with our own farmer network, we know where every crop comes from, how it was grown and how it was handled.

Each batch is checked before it moves to the next stage, so problems are caught early and only clean, consistent, natural products are packed for you.

Our Process

Five checks before it reaches you

  1. 01

    Farm sourcing

    We source only from our own farmer network, where crops are grown with organic practices and we know the land, the grower and the season.

  2. 02

    Harvest checks

    Each lot is inspected at harvest for maturity, cleanliness and freedom from damage before it is accepted.

  3. 03

    Careful processing

    Drying, cleaning, sorting and steam distillation are done in small batches, with each batch kept separate and labelled.

  4. 04

    Batch testing

    Every batch is checked for purity, aroma, colour, moisture and consistency against our reference standards.

  5. 05

    Packing & dispatch

    Approved batches are packed in clean, sealed, well-labelled packaging with batch details for full traceability.

Our Standards

What we check in every batch

Four things matter most to our buyers — so they are the four things we measure every time.

  • Purity

    No added colours, fillers or synthetic fragrances — only the natural product.

  • Freshness

    Aroma, colour and moisture are checked so products reach you at their natural best.

  • Consistency

    Batch-to-batch checks keep quality steady across repeat and bulk orders.

  • Traceability

    Every pack carries batch details, so each order can be traced back to its source.
